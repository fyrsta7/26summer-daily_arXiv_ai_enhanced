### [1] [Agentic AI Networking for Heterogeneous Unmanned Aerial Systems in Low-Altitude Wireless Networks](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.19538)
*Nguyen Duc Minh Quang,Chang Liu,Shuangyang Li,Derrick Wing Kwan Ng*

Main category: cs.AI



Selection reason: 提出LLM编排外循环+MARL内循环的分层多agent架构，用LLM重配置目标与资源优先级，其分层编排思想对agent设计有一般性启发。

TL;DR: 提出一种LLM辅助博弈编排与参数条件化MARL相结合的双环分层架构，使低空无线网络中的异构服务无需重新训练底层策略即可协调共存并适应动态需求。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: LAWNs支持共享三维空域中异构无人机系统的并发服务，导致移动性、连接性与共享网络资源强耦合；异构服务需求不同且时变，形成动态非合作博弈。传统优化与学习型控制器依赖预定义目标，难以自主适应变化的需求与资源优先级。

Method: 提出分层混合LLM-MARL双环架构：外环用LLM辅助的博弈编排解释服务需求与操作者意图，并重配置目标与资源优先级；内环在配置好的博弈下执行去中心化、参数条件化的MARL策略。

Result: 物流监控案例表明，该框架可促进异构服务间的协调共存，并能适应不断变化的运行条件，而无需重新训练底层MARL策略。

Conclusion: 最后讨论面向可扩展、可信且自适应的智能体化LAWNs的关键挑战与研究方向。

中文摘要：低空无线网络（LAWNs）正成为异构无人航空系统的关键基础设施，这些系统在共享的三维空域内支持并发服务。它们的共存造成了机动性、连接性与共享网络资源之间的强耦合，而异构服务提出了不同且时变的需求。这些交互自然构成一个动态非合作博弈，其中运行条件和协调目标均随时间演化。传统优化与基于学习的控制器通常依赖预定义目标，限制了其自主适应不断变化的服务需求和资源优先级的能力。为应对这一挑战，我们提出一种分层混合大语言模型（LLM）—多智能体强化学习（MARL）架构，其组织为双环结构。具体而言，外环适应回路采用LLM辅助的博弈编排来解释服务需求与操作者意图，并重新配置目标与资源优先级；内环则在已配置的博弈下执行去中心化、参数条件化的MARL策略。一个物流监控案例研究说明了所提框架如何促进异构服务之间的协调共存，适应不断变化的运行条件，而无需重新训练底层MARL策略。最后，我们讨论面向可扩展、可信且自适应的智能体化LAWNs的关键挑战与研究方向。

Original abstract: Low-altitude wireless networks (LAWNs) are emerging as a key infrastructure for heterogeneous unmanned aerial systems that support concurrent services within a shared three-dimensional airspace. Their coexistence creates strong coupling among mobility, connectivity, and shared network resources, while heterogeneous services impose distinct and time-varying requirements. These interactions naturally form a dynamic non-cooperative game in which both operating conditions and coordination objectives evolve over time. Conventional optimization and learning-based controllers typically rely on predefined objectives, limiting their ability to adapt autonomously to changing service requirements and resource priorities. To address this challenge, we propose a hierarchical hybrid large language model (LLM)- multi-agent reinforcement learning (MARL) architecture organized as a dual-loop structure. Specifically, an outer adaptation loop employs LLM-assisted game orchestration to interpret service requirements and operator intent, and reconfigure objectives and resource priorities, while an inner loop executes decentralized, parameter-conditioned MARL policies under the configured game. A logistics-monitoring case study illustrates how the proposed framework facilitates coordinated coexistence among heterogeneous services, adapting to evolving operating conditions without retraining the underlying MARL policies. Finally, we discuss key challenges and research directions toward scalable, trustworthy, and adaptive agentic LAWNs.

### [2] [When2Think: Learning Difficulty-Aware Length Control for Efficient Hybrid Reasoning Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.19671)
*Jaejun Shim,HyunJin Kim,Young Jin Kim,JinYeong Bak*

Main category: cs.AI



Selection reason: 面向推理模型的难度感知自适应计算分配与验证器奖励塑形，可迁移到agent的预算/深度控制与反馈式自我改进。

TL;DR: When2Think 将高效推理视为实例自适应计算分配问题，通过 IDAC 奖励塑形和验证器奖励，在无 critic、无奖励模型条件下学习简单题直答/难题长推理，在数学基准上提升准确率并降低 token 消耗。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: LRMs 存在系统性低效：简单题过度思考、难题思考不足；统一长度惩罚和刚性路由会牺牲难题准确率。

Method: 提出 When2Think 后训练框架，动态分配计算；核心 IDAC 利用预计算准确率/token 统计调节推理深度，结合验证器奖励和批次标准化优势，实现稳定的无 critic 优化，无需学习奖励模型或在线参考模型查询。

Result: AIME24：Pass@3 +10.0%，token -27.9%（相对基础模型）；AIME25：40.0% Pass@3，优于压缩与仅路由基线。

Conclusion: 学习何时使用 System 1 (NoThink) 与 System 2 (Think)，改善准确率-效率权衡。

中文摘要：大型推理模型（LRMs）在复杂任务上取得了强劲性能，但表现出系统性的低效：它们常常在简单问题上过度思考，而在困难问题上思考不足。现有基于统一长度惩罚或刚性路由的方法会带来效率税，即在简单实例上减少计算量，却以困难实例上的准确率损失为代价。我们将高效推理建模为实例自适应计算分配问题，并提出 When2Think，一个面向混合推理的后训练框架，可根据问题难度动态分配计算量。我们的方法引入实例级难度感知控制（Instance-level Difficulty-Aware Control, IDAC），一种奖励塑形机制，利用预先计算好的参考统计量（准确率和 token 使用量）来调节推理深度。结合基于验证器的奖励和批次标准化优势，IDAC 实现了稳定的无评论家（critic-free）优化，无需学习奖励模型或在线参考模型查询。When2Think 鼓励在简单实例上直接作答，同时在困难实例上保留扩展推理，从而学习何时使用系统1（NoThink）与系统2（Think）。在数学基准上的实验表明，准确率-效率权衡得到改善：在 AIME24 上，相对于基础模型，Pass@3 提高 10.0%，同时 token 使用量减少 27.9%；在 AIME25 上，When2Think 达到 40.0% Pass@3，优于压缩方法和仅路由基线。

Original abstract: Large Reasoning Models (LRMs) achieve strong performance on complex tasks but exhibit systematic inefficiency: they often overthink easy problems and underthink hard ones. Existing approaches based on uniform length penalties or rigid routing incur an efficiency tax, trading reduced computation on easy instances for accuracy loss on hard instances. We formulate efficient reasoning as an instance-adaptive computation allocation problem and propose When2Think, a post-training framework for hybrid reasoning that dynamically allocates computation based on problem difficulty. Our method introduces Instance-level Difficulty-Aware Control (IDAC), a reward-shaping mechanism that leverages pre-computed reference statistics (accuracy and token usage) to regulate reasoning depth. Combined with verifier-based rewards and batch-wise standardized advantages, IDAC enables stable critic-free optimization without learned reward models or online reference-model queries. When2Think encourages direct answering on easy instances while preserving extended reasoning on hard instances, thereby learning when to use System 1 (NoThink) versus System 2 (Think). Experiments on mathematical benchmarks demonstrate improved accuracy-efficiency trade-offs: on AIME24, Pass@3 increases by 10.0% while token usage is reduced by 27.9% relative to the base model, and on AIME25, When2Think achieves 40.0% Pass@3, outperforming compression and routing-only baselines.

### [3] [Steering Equilibrium Selection in Regularized Self-Play via the Reference Policy](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.19820)
*Luis Leal*

Main category: cs.AI

Comments: 17 pages, 8 figures, 4 tables. Companion to arXiv:2606.28308 and arXiv:2607.17543. Fully reproducible: a single self-contained notebook regenerates every number, table, and figure

Selection reason: 研究正则化自博弈中参考策略对均衡选择的作用，把RLHF式KL锚点重新理解为选择旋钮，对RL训练agent的机制设计有潜在启发。

TL;DR: 通过在期望均衡成员处锚定正则化自对弈的参考策略并加以精炼，可主动选择纳什均衡成员，但需警惕离流形参考、刚性族与边界目标等失效情形。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 正则化自对弈中正则项会悄然打破价值等价均衡的平局（均匀参考下选出最大熵/ I-投影成员），作者由此追问：参考策略能否被有意用来主动选择均衡成员。

Method: 在五个可精确求解博弈与一个二维均衡多面体上，采用精确最优响应与独立种子上的等价性检验，将参考锚定在目标成员并精炼；设置匹配对照臂，比较 Table 与 MLP 引导映射，并考察对抗最优响应时的退化权衡。

Result: 引导达到平均坐标误差 0.007、中位可利用度 5×10⁻⁵（TOST 等价于请求值，±0.05），选择遵循可达加权 I-投影（斜率 0.969 [0.950, 0.987]）；失效方面：固定离流形参考代价 0.08–0.25，刚性/平坦族需更小镜像步，边界目标欠冲，曲率预测边界饱和（秩相关 0.90，p=0.037），内部精度与曲率无关；Table 与 MLP 映射在 ±0.03 内等价，注意力仅是多余种子方差（系统偏移 ≤0.018 且不显著）。

Conclusion: 将参考锚定在期望成员并加以精炼的配方，把 RLHF 式强化学习中的 KL 锚重新诠释为选择旋钮而非单纯的稳定性约束；且引导仅在面对固定非均衡对手时才有意义。

中文摘要：正则化自对弈（regularized self-play）——DeepNash 的 Stratego 对弈背后的方法族——通过向一个缓慢移动的、熵正则化的参考策略 ρ 做最优响应，驱动双人零和策略趋向纳什均衡。当博弈存在一个价值等价的均衡多面体时，正则项会悄然打破平局：在均匀参考下，它选择最大熵成员，即 ρ 在纳什集上的 I-投影。参考策略能否被用来有意地选择均衡？在五个可精确求解的博弈外加一个二维多面体上，使用精确最优响应以及在独立随机种子上进行的等价性检验，将参考锚定在目标成员并加以精炼，可将自对弈引导至该成员，平均坐标误差为 0.007，中位可利用度为 5×10⁻⁵，TOST 等价于请求值，范围在 ±0.05 内；这种锚定在精炼过程中持续存在，并跟随参考而非初始化。选择遵循可达加权 I-投影（斜率为 0.969 [0.950, 0.987]）。我们以同等重视程度报告该叙事失效之处：固定的离流形参考会带来 0.08–0.25 的可利用度代价；刚性或平坦的族需要更小的镜像步长，由预先注册的规则设定；边界目标会欠冲；曲率可预测边界饱和在何处产生影响（秩相关 0.90，p=0.037），而内部精度与曲率无关。在每一个目标上（30 个随机种子），Table 与 MLP 引导映射在 ±0.03 内等价；匹配的对照臂显示，注意力的稳健特征是多余的种子方差，任何系统性偏移被限制在 0.018 以内且不显著。面对最优响应时，选择-稳健性权衡是退化的：引导只在面对固定的、非均衡对手时才重要。该配方——将参考锚定在期望成员并加以精炼——将 RLHF 风格强化学习中的 KL 锚重新诠释为一个选择旋钮，而不仅是稳定性的缰绳。

Original abstract: Regularized self-play -- the family behind DeepNash's Stratego play -- drives a two-player zero-sum policy to a Nash equilibrium by best-responding to a slowly moving, entropy-regularized reference policy $ρ$. When the game has a polytope of value-equivalent equilibria, the regularizer silently breaks the tie: with a uniform reference it selects the maximum-entropy member, the I-projection of $ρ$ onto the Nash set. Can the reference be used to choose the equilibrium on purpose? On five exactly solvable games plus a 2-D polytope, with exact best responses and equivalence tests over independent seeds, anchoring the reference at a target member and refining steers self-play to that member with mean coordinate error 0.007 at median exploitability $5\times10^{-5}$, TOST-equivalent to the request within $\pm0.05$; the anchoring persists through refinement and follows the reference, not the initialization. Selection follows the reach-weighted I-projection (slope 0.969 [0.950, 0.987]). We report with equal emphasis where the story breaks: fixed off-manifold references cost 0.08-0.25 exploitability; stiff or flat families require a smaller mirror step, set by a pre-registered rule; boundary targets undershoot; curvature predicts where boundary saturation bites (rank correlation 0.90, p=0.037) while interior precision is curvature-independent. Table and MLP steering maps are equivalent within $\pm0.03$ at every target (30 seeds); matched control arms show attention's robust signature is excess seed variance, any systematic shift bounded at 0.018 and not significant. Against a best response the selection-robustness trade-off is degenerate: steering matters only against fixed, non-equilibrium opponents. The recipe -- anchor the reference at the desired member and refine -- reinterprets the KL anchor of RLHF-style RL as a selection knob, not only a stability leash.
