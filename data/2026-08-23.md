### [1] [Bounded Sovereignty and the Control Tax: Pricing AI Oversight When the Deployer Does Not Own the Model](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.19216)
*Zhen Wen Lim*

Main category: cs.AI

Comments: 25 pages, 5 figures. Synthetic access-ablation study; not real-world payment-system evidence

Selection reason: 讨论部署者对模型的有限控制权及AI控制协议的可执行性，对agent可靠性与安全控制有启发。

Motivation: 现有AI控制协议常假设部署者可完全监测和控制模型及管道，但受监管组织通过API或托管端点使用模型时，仅能控制业务流程，无法访问模型权重、基础设施、内部日志等，导致许多协议不可执行。因此需要系统研究访问条件如何影响控制协议的实际部署。

Method: 提出四层访问类型学（数据、模型、基础设施、交互层）和协议需求矩阵，定义“主权折扣成本”概念。设计合成访问消融实验，生成135万个合成案例模拟，通过匿名化国家支付基础设施场景进行构念效度检验，对比不同访问条件下的控制效果。

Conclusion: 控制协议不能假设普遍具备完整访问权限，其适用性与访问条件密切相关。通用安全解决方案必须显式声明所需的访问假设，并为缺失访问场景设计成本可见的替代策略，以帮助受监管组织在有限主权下做出风险评估和架构决策。

This paper introduces bounded sovereignty: partial technical and contractual access across the data, model, infrastructure, and interaction layers of the AI stack. It argues that these access conditions determine which control protocols can be executed in practice. The paper contributes a four-layer access typology, a protocol-by-layer requirements matrix, and the concept of sovereignty discount cost: the part of the control tax spent substituting for missing access through contracts, architecture, audit, vendor assurance, residual risk, or reduced system scope. It also reports a synthetic access-ablation experiment over 1.35 million synthetic case simulations and interprets the findings through an anonymised national-payments-infrastructure scenario. The experiment is not real-world payment-system evidence; it is a construct-validity exercise. The results show that complete logs improve diagnosis, a pre-execution gateway enables intervention, trace access and model-version control strengthen post-incident explanation, and scope restriction can improve safety while reducing usefulness. Control protocols proposed as general safety solutions should therefore state their access assumptions explicitly.
