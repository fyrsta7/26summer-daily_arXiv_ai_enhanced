中文摘要：当语言模型被部署时，不存在参考解，因此它写出的推理轨迹是判断其答案的唯一证据。无参考解的替代方法各自以不同方式失败：自评估会降低其本应保护的答案质量，采样一致性衡量的是稳定性而非正确性，扰动研究从人工编写的模板中读出预期答案，评估的是模型而不是轨迹。我们追问：在没有参考解、没有评判模型、不更新权重的情况下，单条轨迹能被验证到什么程度；并发现，对于在 GSM8K 上冻结的 LLaMA-2-7B-chat，检测并不是限制这样一个流程的环节。让轨迹与自身对照达到 0.741 的 AUROC，并因结构性原因止步于此：三分之二的错误轨迹没有陈述错误方程，而是正确执行了一个错误计划；在错误计划导致的失败上，检测器标记错误轨迹的频率反而低于标记正确轨迹。一个由候选答案派生的蜕变探针揭示了许多此类失败：我们将轨迹抽象为作用于问题中数量的程序，扰动一个数量，执行该程序，并就改写后的问题向模型询问一次，从而让每条轨迹提供自己的测试。1,319 个测试问题中有 937 个（71.0%）存在有效扰动；在同时具有可解析首条轨迹的 847 个问题中，受支持轨迹的正确率为 60.6%，而基线正确率为 31.6%，尽管该探针也否定了 45.5% 的正确轨迹。没有一种被测试的策略能在准确率上带来统计显著提升。只有当检测器精确率超过 D/(R+D) 时，一个标记才值得重试，其中 R 和 D 分别是重试修复一条错误轨迹和损害一条正确轨迹的比率；我们的修复率如此之低，以至于在相同重试预算下，即使检测器完美，估计也只能恢复 1,319 个问题中的 66 个。修复，而不是检测，才是未解决的问题。

Original abstract: When a language model is deployed, no reference solution exists, so the reasoning trace it writes is the only evidence for judging its answer. The reference-free substitutes each fail differently: self-assessment degrades the answer it was meant to protect, sampling agreement measures stability rather than correctness, and perturbation studies read the expected answer off a human-written template, grading a model rather than a trace. We ask how far a single trace can be verified with no reference solution, no judge model and no weight update, and find, for a frozen LLaMA-2-7B-chat on GSM8K, that detection is not what limits such a pipeline. Checking a trace against itself reaches an AUROC of 0.741 and stops there for a structural reason: two thirds of wrong traces state no false equation, having executed a mistaken plan correctly, and on wrong-plan failures the detector flags a wrong trace less often than a right one. A candidate-derived metamorphic probe exposes many of these failures: we abstract the trace into a program over the question's quantities, perturb one quantity, execute the program, and ask the model the rewritten question once, so every trace supplies its own test. Valid perturbations exist for 937 of 1,319 test questions (71.0%); among the 847 that also have a parseable first trace, supported traces are correct 60.6% of the time against a 31.6% base rate, although the probe also refutes 45.5% of correct traces. No tested policy yields a statistically significant improvement in accuracy. A flag earns its retry only when detector precision exceeds D/(R+D), where R and D are the rates at which a retry repairs a wrong trace and damages a correct one; our repair rate is so low that, at the same retry budget, even a perfect detector would recover an estimated 66 of 1,319 questions. Repair, not detection, is the open problem.
