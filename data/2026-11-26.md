Original abstract: Autonomous specification engineering requires coding agents to translate functional requirements into executable tests and use the results to discover and validate defects. However, existing benchmarks evaluate coding agents on explicitly specified test generation tasks, commonly providing an issue description, a designated test entry point, or files to inspect, leaving the ability to determine what to test and where to investigate insufficiently evaluated. Relying on such clues bypasses the challenge of mapping requirements to implementations and proactively uncovering unknown defects.
