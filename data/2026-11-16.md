### [4] [One Model, Two Precisions: Does Disagreement Between Quantized Copies Tell You When a Small Model Is Wrong?](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.16478)
*Mohammed Nayeem Khan,Allan Kevin T*

Main category: cs.AI



Selection reason: 利用量化副本间的不一致性进行推理时错误检测，为Agent的可靠性控制与自我反思机制提供了新思路。

TL;DR: 本研究提出利用同一模型不同量化精度（8位与低位）输出结果的不一致性作为错误评分，用于大模型的弃权、升级和重问，该方法无需训练和采样，仅需一次额外前向传播。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>

Method: 在Qwen3-0.6B和Qwen3-1.7B上模拟3-6位的逐组就近舍入量化。让8位模型回答问题，低位模型读取相同提示，计算两者输出的不一致性评分。在8398道多选题上，通过AUROC、风险-覆盖率曲线和ECE评估错误检测能力，并结合置信度特征进行对比。同时对比了高斯噪声、选项顺序打乱的额外前向传播以及路由至大模型等基线方法。

Result: 在Qwen3-0.6B上，最佳不一致性评分AUROC达0.684，结合置信度特征提升0.009；在1.7B模型上提升不显著。研究发现该信号并非量化特有（高斯噪声效果相当），且打乱特征会消除增益。改变选项顺序的额外8位前向传播效果更好（+0.049），但流量增加1/3；而基于自由熵路由至大模型在同等流量下表现最优。0.6B模型经逻辑重校准后ECE表现良好。

Conclusion: 不同精度间的不一致性可作为一种无训练、低成本的错误评分机制用于模型路由和重问，但其效果并非量化独有，且在某些场景下收益有限。研究最终提供了具体的部署方案及该方法的局限性说明。

中文摘要：本地语言模型通常存在多种精度，且已知量化会改变单个答案。本研究探讨了同一权重的两种精度（回答问题的8位副本和读取相同提示的较低精度副本）之间的不一致性，是否可以作为用于弃权、升级和重新提问的逐题错误评分。其代价是仅需对较少字节进行一次额外的前向传播，无需采样和训练。我们在Qwen3-0.6B和Qwen3-1.7B上模拟了带有逐组缩放的就近舍入量化（3到6位，32到256的组），并对来自ARC-Easy、ARC-Challenge、MMLU和HellaSwag子集的8,398道多项选择题进行了评分。错误检测通过留出的一半数据上的AUROC、风险-覆盖率曲线和ECE进行测量，并带有跨问题的配对自举区间。对于Qwen3-0.6B，8位答案的熵达到AUROC 0.682；在开发集的一半上选择的最佳不一致性评分达到0.684（+0.001 [-0.009, +0.011]），将其与置信度特征结合可增加+0.009 [+0.002, +0.015]，主要体现在ARC上。在Qwen3-1.7B上，从熵中选择的评分为-0.001 [-0.009, +0.006]，在测试集的一半上的组合评分为+0.003 [-0.001, +0.006]。在匹配误差能量下的高斯权重噪声与舍入提供的信息量大致相同（仅限0.6B），因此我们没有发现该信号特定于量化的证据，并且在任务和熵单元内打乱特征会消除这种增益。一次改变选项顺序的额外8位前向传播表现更好（比熵高+0.049 [+0.031, +0.067]），但权重流量增加约三分之一；在自由熵基础上路由到更大模型在总流量相同时击败了这两种额外前向传播评分。经过逻辑重新校准后，0.6B的置信度校准在ECE的几个百分点以内。本文提供了一个部署方案以及该方法无效的情况说明。

Original abstract: Local language models often exist at several precisions, and quantization is known to change individual answers. This study asks whether the disagreement between two precisions of the same weights, an 8-bit copy that answers and a lower-precision copy that reads the same prompt, works as a per-question error score for abstention, escalation and re-asking. The cost is one extra forward pass over fewer bytes, with no sampling and no training. We simulate round-to-nearest quantization with per-group scales (3 to 6 bits, groups of 32 to 256) on Qwen3-0.6B and Qwen3-1.7B and score 8,398 multiple-choice questions from ARC-Easy, ARC-Challenge, MMLU and HellaSwag subsets. Error detection is measured by AUROC, risk-coverage curves and ECE on a held-out half, with paired bootstrap intervals over questions. For Qwen3-0.6B, the entropy of the 8-bit answer reaches AUROC 0.682; the best disagreement score, selected on a development half, reaches 0.684 (+0.001 [-0.009, +0.011]), and combining it with the confidence features adds +0.009 [+0.002, +0.015], mostly on ARC. On Qwen3-1.7B the selected score is -0.001 [-0.009, +0.006] from entropy and the combination +0.003 [-0.001, +0.006] on the test half. Gaussian weight noise at matched error energy is about as informative as rounding (0.6B only), so we find no evidence that the signal is specific to quantization, and shuffling the features within task and entropy cells removes the gain. After logistic recalibration the 0.6B confidences are calibrated within a few points of ECE. A deployment recipe and the cases where the method does not help are given.

Motivation: 现有的强化学习安全对齐方法通过奖励权重间接设定安全性。若直接使用拉格朗日乘数法在GRPO中强制执行安全预算，会因评分器均值计算偏差和组内奖励标准化导致的梯度抵消而失效。

Method: 提出双通道约束GRPO：1）基础奖励保留原始分数用于组内比较；2）惩罚和约束估计通过基于评判标签拟合的单调映射进行传递；3）在优势计算中保留拉格朗日乘数，从而修复评分器均值偏差和乘数被抵消的问题。

Result: 在Qwen3-VL-2B模型上，1037个对抗性提示的有害率从标准GRPO的8.9%降至4.8%（未校准预算为7.6%）；400个良性查询的过度拒绝率≤2%；MMBench性能不变。在图像意图有害的任务中改善最大（10.1%降至5.1%），在HADES基准上从28.9%降至12.8%。实验验证了控制器状态的预测及在不同评判器和人工审计下的鲁棒性。

Conclusion: 双通道约束GRPO有效解决了基于预算的拉格朗日乘数法在GRPO中失效的问题，能够在不损害模型通用能力和不增加过度拒绝的前提下，更精确、鲁棒地控制视觉语言模型的安全性。

中文摘要：强化学习可以教导视觉语言模型（VLMs）拒绝有害的图文请求，但其结果的安全性是通过试验运行找到的奖励权重间接设定的。将安全性表述为有害协助率和过度拒绝率的预算，并通过GRPO内部的拉格朗日乘数法以及现成的评分器作为成本来强制执行，是自然的修复方法，但它失败了两次。乘数将评分器的均值与预算进行比较，而为排名构建的评分器会算错均值：不动点分析表明，此时决定乘数是稳定、饱和还是关闭的是均值偏差，而不是预算。此外，标准GRPO对每组内的惩罚奖励进行标准化，这在乘数到达梯度之前就将其抵消了。双通道约束GRPO修复了这两个问题：基础奖励保留原始分数用于组内比较，惩罚和约束估计通过拟合在带有评判标签的训练响应上的单调映射传递，而优势保留乘数。在Qwen3-VL-2B策略上，使用开放4B模型的免训练读取作为评分器，在1,037个对抗性提示上的留出集有害率在标准GRPO下的8.9%降至4.8%，在每个种子中都更低，其中未校准的预算给出7.6%；在400个良性查询上，过度拒绝率保持在最多2%，且MMBench保持不变。当意图存在于图像中时，收益最大（10.1% -> 5.1%），并且在未见过的HADES基准上有所增长（28.9% -> 12.8%）。分数的保序扭曲在每个种子中重现了每个预测的控制器状态，并且结果在拟合于另一个评判标签、图像读取评判和人工审计的映射下依然成立。

Original abstract: Reinforcement learning can teach vision-language models (VLMs) to decline harmful image-text requests, but how safe the result becomes is set indirectly, through reward weights found by trial runs. Stating safety as budgets on the rate of harmful assistance and the rate of over-refusal, enforced by Lagrange multipliers inside GRPO with an off-the-shelf scorer as the cost, is the natural fix, and it fails twice. The multiplier compares the scorer's mean with the budget, and scorers built for ranking get the mean wrong: a fixed-point analysis shows that the mean bias, not the budget, then decides whether the multiplier settles, saturates or switches off. And standard GRPO standardizes the penalized reward within each group, which cancels the multiplier before it reaches the gradient. Two-channel constrained GRPO repairs both: the base reward keeps the raw score for the within-group comparison, the penalty and the constraint estimate pass through a monotone map fitted on judge-labeled training responses, and the advantage keeps the multiplier. On a Qwen3-VL-2B policy with a training-free readout of an open 4B model as the scorer, held-out harm on 1,037 adversarial prompts falls from 8.9% under standard GRPO to 4.8%, lower in every seed, where the uncalibrated budget gives 7.6%; over-refusal stays at most 2% on 400 benign queries and MMBench is unchanged. The gain is largest where the intent sits in the image (10.1% -> 5.1%) and grows on the unseen HADES benchmark (28.9% -> 12.8%). Order-preserving distortions of the scores reproduce each predicted controller state in every seed, and the results hold under a map fitted on another judge's labels, an image-reading judge and a human audit.
