  <summary>Details</summary>

中文摘要：Draft-verify-revise 是一种常见的 LLM 编排模式，用于扩展推理时计算。一个 LLM 起草，第二个对草稿进行批判并提供反馈，第三个利用该反馈修改草稿，形成最终输出。随着上下文在阶段之间级联，不同阶段的 LLM 可能以不同方式解析像“previous”这样的上下文依赖表达式。当这种情况发生时，该表达式会经历指示转移（deictic shift），即其所指发生变化。该现象通过一个包含 10 个基础示例的合成数据集进行了研究，每个示例以三种条件呈现。在保持共享组件不变的情况下，这些条件改变了是草拟阶段 LLM（助手）还是验证阶段 LLM（评分者）正确解析了该表达式，以及修订阶段 LLM（元评估器）需要多少独立推理来确定哪种解读是正确的。来自三个提供方的六种模型在 21 种推理努力配置下进行了测试，使用 e-values 进行序贯检验，包括一个主实验和一个消融实验，后者从评分者的反馈中移除了错误分类标签。另一个单独的 LLM 分析了元评估器对每个错误判定所陈述的理由。平衡准确率（灵敏度和特异度的未加权平均）范围从 0.156（低于随机水平）到接近完美。GPT-5.2 从无推理时的 0.156 上升到其最高推理努力水平时的 0.942，而 Gemini 3 Pro 在每个水平上都保持在 0.94 以上。Gemini 3 Pro 在低推理努力下的得分超过了 GPT-5.2 在 xhigh 推理努力下的得分，而每次试验成本约为后者的 5%。当元评估器出错时，它倾向于依赖表面线索而非操作性推理。实施草拟-验证-修订流水线的上下文工程师应警惕指示转移，并在每个阶段明确预期指称对象。

Original abstract: Draft-verify-revise is a common LLM orchestration pattern for scaling inference-time compute. One LLM drafts, a second critiques the draft and provides feedback, and a third uses that feedback to revise the draft into the final output. As context cascades between stages, LLMs at different stages can resolve a context-dependent expression such as "previous" differently. When that happens, the expression undergoes a deictic shift, a change in what it refers to. This phenomenon was studied with a synthetic dataset of 10 base examples, each rendered in three conditions. Holding the shared components constant, the conditions varied whether the draft stage LLM (the assistant) or the verify stage LLM (the grader) resolved the expression correctly, and how much independent reasoning the revise stage LLM (the meta-evaluator) needed to determine which reading was correct. Six models from three providers were tested across 21 reasoning effort configurations using e-values for sequential testing, in a primary experiment and an ablation experiment that removed error classification labels from the grader's feedback. A separate LLM analyzed the meta-evaluator's stated rationale for each wrong verdict. Balanced accuracy (the unweighted mean of sensitivity and specificity) ranged from 0.156, below chance, to near-perfect. GPT-5.2 rose from 0.156 without reasoning to 0.942 at its highest reasoning effort level, while Gemini 3 Pro stayed above 0.94 at every level. Gemini 3 Pro at low reasoning effort outscored GPT-5.2 at xhigh reasoning effort for roughly 5% of the cost per trial. When the meta-evaluator erred, it tended to rely on surface cues rather than operational reasoning. Context engineers implementing draft-verify-revise pipelines should be wary of deictic shifts and make the intended referent explicit at each stage.
