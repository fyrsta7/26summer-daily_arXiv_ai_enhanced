### [1] [ZeroGate: Trust-Preserving Fast Paths for Governed AI Agent Runtimes](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.25443)
*Zexun Wang*

Main category: cs.AI

Comments: 47 pages, 11 figures, 13 tables. Full research report with an ancillary archive containing reference code, raw experimental records, and offline verification tools

Selection reason: 面向受治理 AI agent 运行时的授权/再验证契约与持久化准入边界，涉及 agent 动作可靠性与安全控制，对 agent 执行框架设计有迁移价值。

TL;DR: ZeroGate 将精确动作授权与持久本地准入分离，通过短时效 ActionPass、运行时重建、本地闸门校验与 SQLite 原子消耗实现可重验证的准入边界；它只提供条件性决策保持，不保证现势性或远程恰好一次；在 Azure Blob 评估中，预备执行降低了准入到分派的 p95，但完整生命周期更慢，因此不是净加速。

Motivation: 提前授权可缩短分派边界，但会引入动作载荷、权限或相关状态变化的风险；需要在不夸大为新密码原语或通用性能前沿的前提下，明确授权成本支付位置和可重验证的准入契约。

Method: ZeroGate 分离精确动作批准与持久本地准入：签发者签署短时效 ActionPass，可信运行时适配器重建最终动作，本地闸门校验绑定并消耗 nonce；SQLite 事务原子耦合 nonce 消耗、配额更新与准入回执。评估包括人工语义测试夹具、受控并发与崩溃实验，以及 Azure Blob 中通过同一签发者和闸门对比同步与预备执行。

Original abstract: Moving authorization earlier can shorten an agent's dispatch boundary without removing authorization work. It can also admit an action whose payload, authority, or relevant state has changed. ZeroGate separates exact-action approval from durable local admission: an issuer signs a short-lived ActionPass, and a trusted runtime adapter reconstructs the final action before a local gate checks its binding and consumes its nonce. A SQLite transaction couples nonce consumption, applicable quota updates, and an admission receipt. Prepared mean complete lifecycle is longer at every level: the boundary improvement is not a net speedup. The contribution is an explicit revalidation contract, a durable reference boundary, and an auditable comparison of where authorization cost is paid, not a new cryptographic primitive or a universal performance frontier.

Motivation: 在治理场景中，需要从可观测属性中确定足以决定声明判定的最小契约，并区分单独不可或缺属性与联合充分属性，以便在成本或基数约束下选择可授权契约。

Method: 形式化有限可达状态模型、确定性声明判定和候选可观测属性；定义观测契约与权威契约；在可穷举时枚举所有包含极小充分契约，否则用 SAT/MaxSAT 编码合成最小基数或最小成本契约并直接验证充分性；以 AuthorityBench 在三个领域比较四个基线，并测量可辨别族扩展性。

Result: 在构造的代码/云领域中，单独不可或缺核心不充分且存在两个不同约简，预注册成本模型精确区分它们；在第二个更大构造领域中同样模式出现，但成本模型无法区分，出现完全解释的成本并列；穷举在注册超时内不可行，SAT/MaxSAT 远低于一秒求解，MaxSAT 无基数优势；仅声明基线在所有领域均不恰好充分；成功时给出检查摘要而非可移植证书。

Conclusion: 最小充分治理上下文可通过枚举或 SAT/MaxSAT 合成并经过充分性检查；成本模型可区分约简或如实报告并列；当前范围聚焦编译器，不提供可移植证书，端到端案例研究为注册后续工作。

中文摘要：我们将本文推导并认证的对象称为最小充分治理上下文：给定有限可达状态模型、确定性声明的判定以及候选可观测属性，我们计算充分观测集，区分哪些属性单独不可或缺与哪些契约共同充分，并在基数或声明成本目标下从充分契约中进行选择。观测契约是一组候选属性，其取值在每个可达状态上决定声明的判定；权威契约是在某个目标下选出并绑定到门模式的契约。在穷举枚举可承受时，我们合成每个包含极小的充分契约；否则，通过 SAT/MaxSAT 编码合成最小基数或最小成本契约，并直接检查充分性。在一个构造的代码/云领域中，单独不可或缺的核心作为观测契约并不充分，且存在两个不同的约简；一个预注册的成本模型将它们精确区分。在第二个更大的构造领域中，同样的模式再次出现，但该领域的成本模型不能区分这些备选方案：一个被完全解释的成本并列，按发现如实报告。我们测量可辨别族的扩展性：在注册超时内确认穷举枚举不可行，而 SAT/MaxSAT 合成在远低于一秒内求解；MaxSAT 相对普通 SAT 未显示出可测量的基数优势。AuthorityBench 在三个领域上比较四个基线；仅声明的基线在任何领域上都不恰好充分。每个选中的契约都进行充分性检查，失败时给出反例，成功时给出检查摘要而非可移植证书——这是双范围表中以编译器为重点的范围；一个独立指定的端到端案例研究是注册的后续工作，本文不声称完成。

Original abstract: We call the object this paper derives and certifies a minimal sufficient governance context: given a finite reachable-state model, a deterministic declared verdict, and candidate observable attributes, we compute sufficient observation sets, distinguish attributes that are individually indispensable from contracts that are jointly sufficient, and select among sufficient contracts under a cardinality or declared-cost objective. An observation contract is a set of candidate attributes whose values determine the declared verdict on every reachable state; an authority contract is one selected under an objective and bound to a gate schema. We synthesize every inclusion-minimal sufficient contract where exhaustive enumeration is affordable, and a minimum-cardinality or minimum-cost contract by SAT/MaxSAT encoding otherwise, checking sufficiency directly. On a constructed code/cloud domain, the individually-indispensable core is not sufficient as an observation contract and two distinct reducts exist; a preregistered cost model separates them exactly. On a second, larger, constructed domain, the same pattern recurs, but that domain's cost model does not separate the alternatives: a fully explained cost tie, reported as found. We measure discernibility-family scaling where exhaustive enumeration is confirmed infeasible within a registered timeout, while SAT/MaxSAT synthesis solves in well under a second; MaxSAT showed no measured cardinality advantage over plain SAT. AuthorityBench compares four baselines across three domains; the declared-only baseline is not exactly sufficient on any. Every selected contract is checked for sufficiency, with a counterexample on failure and a check summary, not a portable certificate, on success -- the compiler-focused scope of a two-scope table; an independently specified end-to-end case study is registered follow-up work, not claimed here.
