中文摘要：可重复且分析稳健的研究发现对科学进步至关重要，但评估这些属性通常需要昂贵的人工工作。我们研究LLM智能体是否能够帮助自动化复现和稳健性分析，使用一个基于监测、流行病学和最终结果（SEER）数据库的医学案例研究；SEER数据库是美国广泛使用的癌症登记数据库，支撑着数万项已发表研究。首先，我们提出SEER-Repro，一个包含20篇已发表的基于SEER的论文以及复现其发现所需数据和资源的基准。其次，我们创建一个AI智能体，成功复现了2,190个原子主张中的77.4%，同时为进一步分析提供可执行基线。第三，我们开发一个智能体分析流水线，通过识别每个发现背后的分析选择、提出可辩护的替代方案，并重新运行分析以确定这些选择是否影响结论，来检验医学发现的稳健性。在SEER-Repro中的已发表论文中，我们的流水线在9.5%的完全复现发现中识别出可能具有重要影响的分析选择，并将其标记供专家审查。这些结果表明，LLM智能体如何通过复现发现、系统检验其稳健性，并引导专家关注可能具有重要影响的分析选择，来支持科学审查。

Original abstract: Reproducible and analytically robust research findings are essential to scientific progress, but evaluating these properties typically requires costly manual effort. We investigate whether LLM agents can help automate reproduction and robustness analysis, using a medical case study grounded in the Surveillance, Epidemiology, and End Results (SEER) database, a widely used U.S. cancer registry underlying tens of thousands of published studies. First, we introduce SEER-Repro, a benchmark of 20 published SEER-based papers together with the data and resources required to reproduce their findings. Second, we create an AI agent that successfully reproduces 77.4% of 2,190 atomic claims, while providing executable baselines for further analysis. Third, we develop an agentic analysis pipeline to test the robustness of the medical findings by identifying analytical choices underlying each finding, proposing defensible alternatives, and rerunning the analysis to determine whether those choices affect the conclusion. Across published papers in SEER-Repro, our pipeline identifies potentially consequential analytical choices in 9.5% of fully reproduced findings and flags them for expert review. These results show how LLM agents can support scientific review by reproducing findings, systematically testing their robustness, and directing expert attention toward potentially consequential analytical choices.
