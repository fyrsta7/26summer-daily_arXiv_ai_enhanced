### [1] [Route-and-Generate: KL-Regularized Router Improvement for Reasoning Routing in LLMs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.30351)

Our approach decouples reasoning effort allocation from answer generation by introducing a routing policy that allocates a fixed sampling budget across multiple reasoning formats, while the base model is optimized with GRPO using per-sample rewards. To promote efficient reasoning, we employ a rank-based reward that favors shorter correct solutions, and update the routing policy via a closed-form KL-regularized policy improvement step distilled into a neural router.
