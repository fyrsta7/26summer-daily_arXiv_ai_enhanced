中文摘要：大语言模型（LLMs）越来越多地用于生物医学和认知神经科学，但它们在异构知识图谱（KGs）上进行可靠推理的能力仍不清楚。我们在五个知识图谱上对五个LLM（GPT-5.1、Gemini 2.0 Pro、Llama 3、Qwen和BioGPT）进行基准测试：DrugBank、NeuroSynth、Cognitive Atlas、Brain Map和GDPKG。我们引入一个多层级查询数据集，以在知识图谱感知（图接地）和知识图谱不可知条件下评估推理深度和事实准确性。标注者间一致性为实质性，Cohen's kappa为0.781（范围：0.753-0.807），Fleiss' kappa为0.77。结果表明，通用模型，尤其是GPT-5.1和Gemini 2.0 Pro，在多跳和高层次推理任务上通常优于领域特定模型，尽管性能会随查询复杂度增加而下降。知识图谱接地降低了所有评估模型的幻觉率。与我们的初始假设相反，在没有图接地的情况下，领域特定模型在专门任务上并不一致地优于通用模型。这些发现凸显了将广泛的语言模型能力与结构化领域知识相结合的价值。我们根据任务复杂度和计算约束，为选择LLM-KG集成策略提供实用指导，并讨论用于个性化医疗、认知障碍诊断和可解释AI的混合神经符号架构。

Original abstract: Large language models (LLMs) are increasingly used in biomedical and cognitive neuroscience, but their ability to reason reliably over heterogeneous knowledge graphs (KGs) remains unclear. We benchmark five LLMs (GPT-5.1, Gemini 2.0 Pro, Llama 3, Qwen, and BioGPT) across five KGs: DrugBank, NeuroSynth, Cognitive Atlas, Brain Map, and GDPKG. We introduce a multitier query dataset to evaluate reasoning depth and factual accuracy under KG-aware (graph-grounded) and KG-agnostic conditions. Inter-annotator agreement was substantial, with Cohen's kappa of 0.781 (range: 0.753-0.807) and Fleiss' kappa of 0.77. Results show that general-purpose models, particularly GPT-5.1 and Gemini 2.0 Pro, generally outperform domain-specific models on multi-hop and high-level reasoning tasks, although performance declines as query complexity increases. KG grounding reduces hallucination rates across all evaluated models. Contrary to our initial hypothesis, domain-specific models do not consistently outperform general-purpose models on specialized tasks without graph grounding. These findings highlight the value of combining broad language-model capabilities with structured domain knowledge. We provide practical guidance for selecting LLM-KG integration strategies based on task complexity and computational constraints, and discuss hybrid neurosymbolic architectures for personalized medicine, cognitive disorder diagnosis, and explainable AI.
