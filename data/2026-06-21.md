### [1] [Hypothesis-Driven Skill Optimization for LLM Agents](http://arxiv.org/abs/2606.22330v1)
*Fangxin Shang,Yehui Yang*

Main category: cs.AI

Selection reason: cs.SE 论文按全量策略收录。

TL;DR: 提出HDSO框架，通过假设驱动的技能验证和渐进式披露，在无训练条件下提升LLM代理性能，并能抵抗噪声反馈。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 外部技能可改善LLM代理，但从稀疏或噪声轨迹中蒸馏技能存在风险，因为反思可能包含无用或不可靠的规则，需要一种无训练的可审计框架来安全地积累技能。

Method: HDSO框架包含冻结的curator和executor：curator观察执行痕迹，提出可证伪假设并设计验证计划，实例化为技能包，通过对照/处理执行验证，仅整合支持的技能；executor通过渐进式披露使用技能，未选择时回退到自身路径。

Conclusion: HDSO为冻结行动代理提供了可审计的技能生命周期，避免了无约束记忆积累，通过假设驱动验证在噪声下保持鲁棒性，并支持技能转移。

A plausible reflection may encode a useful procedure, a spurious shortcut, or a rule that the target executor cannot reliably follow. Transfer and heterogeneous-pair diagnostics further show that validated repositories can be useful beyond the run that produced them, but cross-model curation succeeds only when curator diagnosis, executor capability, and validation evidence align. HDSO provides an auditable skill lifecycle for frozen action agents rather than an unconstrained memory accumulation procedure.
