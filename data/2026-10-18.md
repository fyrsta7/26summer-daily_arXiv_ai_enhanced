### [1] [Evaluating Model Capabilities After Benchmark Saturation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.20667)

Main category: cs.AI



Selection reason: 提出用已饱和基准的完整解题轨迹（含推理与工具调用通道）而非分数来评估模型能力，对 agent 轨迹利用与长程任务评测方法有可迁移启发。

  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 基准分数一旦饱和便失去区分模型和支撑安全案例与前沿政策的效用，而题目记录包含比分数更丰富的信息，因此需研究能否利用饱和基准的记录继续评估能力。

Method: 利用历史基准与模型发布回顾性模拟饱和，测试仅使用饱和基准中正确解答记录的方法能否预测留出未饱和题目上的能力；比较成对评判、单记录绝对评分及基于确定性记录特征或嵌入的预测。

中文摘要：AI能力的快速进步正在导致基准测试饱和。一旦若干模型接近满分，基准测试就失去区分它们的能力，也随之失去为AI安全案例和前沿政策提供信息的效用。然而，基准测试题目所产生的信息远多于我们通常从中提取的分数：每个模型如何解决任务的完整记录。我们研究这些记录能否在基准分数已经饱和之后仍用于评估模型能力。我们利用历史基准和模型发布回顾性地模拟饱和，并测试仅访问饱和基准中正确解答记录的协议能否预测在留出的、未饱和题目上测得的能力。我们评估了从模型记录中提取信息的代表性当代方法：成对评判、单记录绝对评分，以及基于确定性记录特征或嵌入的预测。我们发现，饱和记录保留了超出基准准确率的能力信息。在我们评估的协议中，成对评判表现最佳，与仅使用饱和基准分数的基线相比，将预测误差降低了5.3倍。这种与能力相关的信息分布在记录各部分中，但集中在特定信息通道，例如推理和工具调用。然而，协议的预测能力并不总能跨分布偏移泛化——例如会低估2025年初引入的首批推理模型的能力。因此，饱和基准在其任务记录中保留了预测能力的信号，但要延长现有基准的寿命，需要旨在稳健提取这些信号的方法。

We simulate saturation retrospectively using historical benchmark and model releases, and test whether protocols that access only correctly solved transcripts from saturated benchmarks can predict capabilities measured on held-out, unsaturated items. We evaluate representative contemporary approaches for extracting information from model transcripts: pairwise judging, single-transcript absolute scoring, and predictions from deterministic transcript features or embeddings. We find that saturated transcripts retain capability information beyond benchmark accuracy. Amongst our evaluated protocols, pairwise judging performs best, reducing prediction error by 5.3 times compared to a baseline using only saturated benchmark scores. This capability-relevant information is distributed across transcript sections, but concentrated in specific channels of information such as reasoning and tool-calls. However, the predictive power of protocols does not always generalise across distribution shifts -- for example by underestimating the capabilities of the first reasoning models introduced in early 2025. Saturated benchmarks thus retain capability-predicting signal in their task transcripts, but extending the lifetime of existing benchmarks requires methods designed to extract these signals robustly.

  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 仅用数值误差指导符号回归搜索，可能偏向精确的替代模型而忽略正确结构，因此需要引入学习到的结构指导。

Original abstract: Symbolic regression searches for an equation that explains observed data. Numerical error is commonly used to guide the search, but it alone can favor an accurate surrogate over a candidate with the right structure. We construct structural supervision signal using tree edit distance (TED), accounting for commutative operators and alternative expression forms. A verifier first aligns numerical observations with the formulas, then learns to score a candidate's structural similarity to the target formula. At search time, the target formula is hidden and the verifier complements numerical error in parent selection. An analyst then uses verifier feedback and diagnostic tools to propose revisions, which a generator converts into refined expressions. On LLM-SRBench, L2G-SR achieves state-of-the-art numerical fit, equation level accuracy and TED across the evaluated LLM backbones and domains. L2G-SR demonstrates the value of learned structural guidance for LLM-based symbolic regression.

### [3] [Failure Is the Mother of Success: Recovering Supervision with Grounding Hindsight](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.21018)
*Sheldon Yu,Tong Yu,Nikki Lijing Kuang,Zhendong Chu,Zhenwei Tang,Sungchul Kim,Lina Yao,Julian McAuley,Junda Wu*

Main category: cs.AI



Selection reason: 将失败 rollout 转为自生成纠错笔记并通过重放成功率筛选与蒸馏，提供了从失败轨迹中提取监督的可迁移反思/自我改进机制。

TL;DR: RSHD 将 GRPO 中失败的 rollout 转化为经重放检验的纠正性笔记，并把成功重放蒸馏回纯提示策略，从而在全失败组中恢复监督，且部署无需额外推理。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: GRPO 在某个问题的所有 rollout 均失败时，既没有成功范例也没有非零组相对优势，导致模型无法从最需要学习的问题中获得学习信号；但失败推理仍可提供下一步尝试的线索。

Method: RSHD 让策略在无参考答案条件下为每次失败尝试写纠正性笔记，再带着笔记重解问题；先筛除直接陈述答案的笔记，仅当重放成功率高于原始组时接纳该笔记，并将被接纳的重放蒸馏到纯提示策略中。训练只更新策略，评判仅依赖结果验证器。

Result: 跨模型家族、模型规模和两个训练领域，RSHD 在域内基准上持续优于 GRPO，且性能增益可迁移到域外评估集。

Conclusion: RSHD 利用重放验证从失败尝试中恢复监督，适用于全失败组和混合组中的失败，并保持部署时无需笔记或额外推理。

中文摘要：当一个问题上的每次 rollout 都失败时，GRPO 既得不到成功范例，也得不到非零的组相对优势，因此它恰恰无法从最需要学习的问题中学到任何东西。然而，失败的推理仍能提示下一步该尝试什么。我们提出 Replay-Selected Hindsight Distillation（RSHD），它在 GRPO 训练期间将失败尝试转化为一种额外的、经过检验的监督来源。策略在看不到参考答案或解答的情况下，为每次失败尝试写一条纠正性笔记，然后在上下文中带上该笔记重新求解该问题。只有在筛选掉直接陈述答案的笔记后，并且该笔记对应的重放成功率高于原始组时，该笔记才被接纳；被接纳的重放会被蒸馏到纯提示策略中。由于接纳由新的成功结果而非失败 rollout 本身决定，该过程可以从所有尝试均失败的组中恢复监督，并且同样适用于混合组中的失败。只训练策略，唯一的评判者是结果验证器，部署时不需要笔记或额外推理。跨模型家族和规模以及两个训练领域，RSHD 在域内基准上持续优于 GRPO，且增益可迁移到域外评估集。

Original abstract: When every rollout for a problem fails, GRPO receives neither a successful exemplar nor a nonzero group-relative advantage, so it learns nothing from exactly the problems it most needs to learn. The failed reasoning can nevertheless suggest what to try next. We present Replay-Selected Hindsight Distillation (RSHD), which turns failed attempts into an additional, tested source of supervision during GRPO training. The policy writes a corrective note about each failed attempt without seeing a reference answer or solution, then re-solves the problem with the note in context. A note is admitted only if its replays succeed more often than the original group did, after screening for notes that state the answer, and an admitted replay is distilled into the plain-prompt policy. Because admission is decided by fresh successes rather than by the failed rollout itself, the procedure can recover supervision from groups in which every attempt failed, and it applies equally to failures in mixed groups. Only the policy is trained, the only judge is the outcome verifier, and deployment requires no notes or extra inference. Across model families and sizes and two training domains, RSHD consistently improves over GRPO on in-domain benchmarks, and the gains carry over to out-of-domain evaluation sets.
